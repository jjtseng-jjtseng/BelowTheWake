All new representational geometry for the visual upgrade pass was made in
Blender 5.1. No generated illustrations or hand-drawn placeholder props are
used. Equipment pictures are Blender renders of the actual models.

Downloaded source meshes:
- Kenney Watercraft Kit tug: adapted cabin windows, corrected bow direction.
- Quaternius Henry: original rigged animated character, fitted diving gear.
- Max Hancock Crane: adapted upper works with new chain (CC BY 3.0).

Blender-authored additions: fitted wooden wheel, gauges, throttle and cabin
details; roof sonar; deck cargo locker; rigged diving mask, harness, regulator,
air tanks, cutter, reinforced suit pieces, fins and bag; salvage lift bag.
The tug's cockpit uses the downloaded boat and a fitted Blender interior.

Editable .blend files and reproducible Blender scripts are supplied separately
in the Blender-assets archive. Source-specific licenses are beside the models.
Interface icons are Lucide SVGs; their ISC/MIT text is in icons/LICENSE.txt.

Expansion: Quaternius Spear fitted/normalized in Blender as a harpoon bolt,
with a Blender-authored brass swivel, wood bed, navy pedestal and rope reel.
Quaternius Shark and Tentacle downloaded with their original skeletal clips.
No monster meshes were generated or approximated with primitive shapes.
